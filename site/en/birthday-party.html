Birthday parties: a painting workshop guests take home

A table, some brushes and a plaster figurine for each child: the party becomes a creative workshop, and every guest leaves with their own creation as a party favour.

💬 Plan my party on WhatsAppSee the models

Why a painting workshop at a birthday?

Between the cake and the games there’s always a moment when kids get restless. A plaster painting workshop brings them together around one table: everyone picks colours, concentrates, compares with friends… and calm returns. It works across ages: little ones paint the big areas, older kids take care of the details.

Two ways to use it

🎨 During the party

Lay a tablecloth, put a kit in front of each guest and start the workshop. Keep an adult nearby for young children (small parts and paint).

🎁 As a party favour

Instead of a bag of sweets, each guest leaves with a complete kit to paint at home – a souvenir that lasts longer than a balloon.

What’s in each kit

40 DH per kit (2 large, 3 medium-sized or 5 small plaster figurines + paints + brushes). Everything is included. The models on the site are just a sample: we have many more figurines and new kits arrive regularly.

How to order for a birthday

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with the number of children, their age, the date and your city.
  2. Pick a theme: we send you photos of available models.
  3. Receive your kits: delivery anywhere in Morocco, delivery price confirmed before you order.

Order a few days ahead, especially for many guests or a custom request.

FAQ

How many kits do I need?

Usually one kit per child. Tell us the number of guests and their age and we’ll suggest the right quantity.

Can I choose a theme?

Yes: cars, characters, animals, fast food, a girls/boys mix… Tell us on WhatsApp and we’ll send photos of the available models.

Is there a price for several kits?

A kit is 40 DH. For large quantities we offer a volume discount on quote.

Do you deliver to my city?

Yes, anywhere in Morocco. The delivery price depends on the city and quantity and is confirmed on WhatsApp.
