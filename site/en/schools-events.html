Schools, nurseries & events: a ready-made painting workshop

A hands-on activity ready for any group: every participant gets a complete kit, and there’s nothing else to prepare.

💬 Ask for a group quoteSee the models

Who is it for?

  • Schools and nurseries: class workshops, end-of-year parties, school fairs.
  • Summer camps and holiday clubs: a calm activity for groups, in large quantities.
  • Companies: family days, year-end parties, gifts for employees’ children.
  • Shops, cafés, kids’ spaces: run a workshop or resell our kits.

Why it works with groups

Painting a figurine takes focus and patience. Everyone works on their own piece at their own pace and leaves with something to be proud of. Set-up is simple: a protected table, one kit per participant and water to rinse brushes.

What you get

40 DH per kit (2 large, 3 medium-sized or 5 small plaster figurines + paints + brushes). For bulk orders we offer a volume discount on quote. Theme, mixed models, packaging for the occasion: tell us what you need.

FAQ

Can you supply a whole class or a large group?

Yes. Tell us the number of children, their age and the date and we’ll prepare a quote with a volume discount.

Is it suitable for young children?

Yes, with an adult present: kits contain small parts and paint.

Can models be mixed?

Yes, we can vary the models or prepare a girls/boys mix for your group.

Do you deliver outside Casablanca?

We deliver anywhere in Morocco; the delivery price depends on the city and quantity.
