💛 About us

A family brand, made in Morocco

Miniakits (Minia Kits) was born at home, around a table covered in brushes. Like many parents, we wanted to give our kids real time away from screens: calm, creative, and something to be proud of.

So we put together complete kits ourselves — plaster figurines, paints and brushes — so every child can create, imagine and colour their own little masterpiece. Today we share them with families, schools and parties all over Morocco.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a kit?

One price: 40 DH per kit (2 large, 3 medium-sized or 5 small figurines + paints + brushes). For large quantities we offer volume discounts: just ask us for a quote.

Do you deliver to my city?

Yes, we deliver anywhere in Morocco. Delivery in Casablanca: 20 DH. Other cities: price depends on the destination and carrier.

How do I order?

Everything happens on WhatsApp at 07 00 56 33 50. Pick your kit, send a message, and we take care of the rest.

What age is it for?

There's no age limit to creating! Children, teens or adults: everyone can enjoy it, for fun, to relax or as an introduction to painting. It's a perfect activity to share with family or friends. Young children should be supervised by an adult (small parts and paint).

Can you customise for a birthday or a school?

Yes! Theme, quantity, a mix for girls and boys, guest gifts, a workshop during the party… Tell us your idea.

Can I choose the models?

Yes. The kits shown here are only part of our range, and we add new ones regularly. Ask us about other models on WhatsApp and we'll send you photos.
