The gift that won’t end up at the back of the cupboard

A gift you make before you keep it: the child (or teen, or adult) paints the figurine, then proudly displays it.

💬 Choose a gift on WhatsAppSee the models

Why give a painting kit?

Toys pile up and many are forgotten within days. A painting kit is enjoyed twice: the creative, screen-free moment, then the finished piece you keep. It’s also a gift to share – a parent, sibling or cousin often sits down to paint too.

For which occasions?

  • Birthdays: as the main gift or one kit per guest (see birthday parties).
  • Ramadan and Eid: a gift that keeps kids busy during the holidays.
  • School success, end of year: a reward that isn’t a screen.
  • For a teen or adult: some relaxing me-time (see teens & adults).
  • No reason at all: a rainy weekend or a visit to the grandparents.

Choosing the right model

Each kit contains 2 large, 3 medium-sized or 5 small plaster figurines, paints and brushes, for 40 DH per kit. Younger children often prefer large, simple shapes; older ones enjoy fine details. Cars, characters, animals, fast food… the photos are just a sample: ask us for more models.

Buying for several children? Ask us for a price on multiple kits. Delivery anywhere in Morocco.
