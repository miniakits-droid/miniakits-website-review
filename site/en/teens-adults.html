Teens & adults: painting as a relaxing break

You don’t need to be a child (or an artist) to enjoy painting. A figurine, a few colours and an hour without notifications is often all it takes to unwind.

💬 Order my kitSee the models

A way to switch off

Painting a figurine takes just enough attention to put everything else aside. Pick your palette, fill the big areas, then come back for the details. Many people find it soothing, like adult colouring – with a 3D piece to keep or give at the end.

With friends, as a couple or solo

  • An evening with friends: a kit each, one table, some music, and compare results at the end.
  • With family: parents and teens around the same table, each at their own level.
  • Solo: a calm introduction to painting without buying complicated supplies.

Going further

Try gradients, shading with a darker tone, or a tiny white dot to make a character’s eyes shine.
